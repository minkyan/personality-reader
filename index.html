Saved profiles

The ten facets (“aspects”) and their correlations follow DeYoung, Quilty & Peterson (2007), with values rounded and approximate. Regional offsets are modeled on the T-score approach of Schmitt et al. (2007), with rank orderings informed by McCrae & Terracciano (2005), Allik & McCrae (2004) and, for US regions, Rentfrow et al. (2013). They are illustrative: real regional differences are modest (typically 0.2–0.5 SD), cross-cultural self-reports are noisy, and any individual differs from their region far more than regions differ from each other.

The interest, career, friend and partner estimates are a hand-built synthesis of general findings (for example, openness with intellectual and artistic pursuits, conscientiousness with job performance, partner emotional stability and agreeableness with relationship satisfaction). They are informed approximations, not direct measurements; similarity in personality predicts friendship and relationship satisfaction only weakly.

Life-outcome links follow the Life Outcomes of Personality Replication project (Soto, 2019) and Roberts et al. (2007); the individual effect sizes shown are rounded blends and deliberately conservative.

Music links draw on Rentfrow & Gosling (2003), the MUSIC-model work that followed it, and Spotify listening data (Anderson et al., 2021). Genre correlations differ across studies and cultures and are small, so the genre lists are cautious blends.

Profile codes are just your ten facet percentiles in a compact form (with a check character), so they can be shared by copy and paste. Saved profiles are kept only in this browser’s local storage.

Each chapter shows how solid its evidence is. “Solid” means descriptive facts or well-replicated correlations; “moderate” means real but small effects; “tentative” means my own synthesis or modeled values that are least certain.

The quizzes are informal and use approximate norms. For a validated assessment, try a published inventory (such as the BFI-2 or the Big Five Aspects Scales) and enter those percentiles above. This page runs entirely in your browser; scores are stored only in this browser’s local storage.
